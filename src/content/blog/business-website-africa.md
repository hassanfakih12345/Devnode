Across Africa, from Kinshasa to Lagos to Nairobi, many businesses run entirely on Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp. That makes sense: it's free and your customers are already there. But relying only on social media leaves real opportunities on the table.

## 1. Credibility with bigger clients

When a company, NGO or international partner considers working with you, one of the first things they do is search for your website. A professional site with your services, past work and clear contact details tells them you are an established business.

## 2. You own it

A social media account can be restricted, hacked or lose reach overnight when the platform changes its rules. Your website and your domain belong to you.

## 3. Customers can find you on Google

People search on Google for things like "perfume shop Kinshasa" or "construction company Lubumbashi". Social media pages rarely show up for these searches. A website with the right content can.

## 4. Everything in one place

Your services, prices, photos, location and WhatsApp button, organised and easy to find, instead of scattered across old posts.

## 5. It works with WhatsApp, not against it

A good website doesn't replace WhatsApp. It sends more people to it. Every DevNode website has WhatsApp buttons so visitors can message you in one tap.
